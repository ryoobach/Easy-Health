## HbA1c estimation design discussion — proposal only
- Discussed a daily 8-slot pattern: waking/fasting, breakfast pre/post (2h), lunch pre/post (2h), dinner pre/post (2h), and bedtime. The fasting and breakfast-pre slots may be the same measurement and must never be double-counted.
- Proposed using the user's recent ~30-day meal-specific pre/post differences (breakfast, lunch, dinner) to inform limited statistical estimates for missing periods, while preserving actual readings separately from estimated values.
- Exercise and ordinary lifestyle variation are naturally represented in a sufficiently representative month of paired observations; they must not be separately adjusted again without a clear model, to avoid double-counting.
- This is a design proposal, not a validated clinical algorithm. It is NOT implemented in this 1.40 source change. Existing time-weighted glucose estimate remains unchanged until a validated method, confidence rules, and tests are agreed. Missing readings must not be silently inserted as if measured.

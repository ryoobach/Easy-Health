## Implemented changes

### Measurement subject profile
- Name, sex, age, and height are required.
- Incomplete profiles are prompted for completion before continuing to use the app.
- Removed baseline weight from the profile. Weight remains a measurement record so historical changes can be tracked.
- Profile serialization ignores legacy `weightKg` data.

### Calendar and personal records
- Renamed the calendar tab to `일정·일기`.
- Selecting a date shows all records for that date.
- The new-record area stays above existing records.
- Multiple hospital appointments, health-journal entries, and simple memos are supported on the same date.
- Existing cards open in edit mode.
- Appointment status is displayed as `확인`.
- Hospital appointments require a reminder choice: 1 day, 2 hours, 30 minutes, or 1 day + 2 hours before the appointment.
- Health journals have no reminder. Simple memos can optionally have a reminder.
- Reminder notifications use the shared appointment receiver with a title/body payload.

### Bluetooth device registration and import
- Registered devices are shown without checkboxes. Long-press opens a delete confirmation.
- Deleting a device removes its registration/address mapping, but does not remove historical vital records.
- Newly discovered supported Bluetooth devices are shown with checkboxes and are added only after pressing `선택 기기 추가`.
- BLE discovery recognizes Contour, CareSens/i-SENS, OMRON, and CAS/BFA device-name families.
- The Records-tab action retains the Bluetooth icon and uses the exact label `측정값 자동 가져오기`.
- Contour Plus ONE and CareSens Dual are the two glucose meters exposed for automatic import. OMRON and CAS automatic data import remains disabled until their GATT protocols are verified.
